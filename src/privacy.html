Privacy policy

Queue Trainer · last updated 12 October 2026

Short version

Queue Trainer does not collect, send or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What is stored, and where

  • Your main role, your champion pool, your levels and any tasks you flag are stored only on your own device.
  • Deleting the app (or clearing the site data in your browser) deletes all of it.

Network requests

The app ships with everything it needs. In the web version it may load champion images from Riot Games' public Data Dragon service; that request contains no information about you beyond what any web request does (such as your IP address, handled by Riot under its own privacy policy).

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
